ANALYTIC SCAFFOLD IRAN · 31 PROVINCES v1 · NOV 2026

A subnational socioeconomic-stress heatmap of the Islamic Republic of Iran — every province scored on eight indicators across two axes: structural fragility (poverty, joblessness, human-capital drain, fiscal dependence) and acute 2024–26 shocks (currency & inflation, water/power/environment, conflict & strike, fuel & energy-subsidy). A Digital Public-Sphere Signals layer adds a second lens: what Iran's open platforms reveal about economic distress, and how much state filtering hides. A Communication Channels accounting maps the stratified information environment itself — every documented channel's status, reach, shutdown-resilience and state visibility.

Digital public-sphere signals — reading the open platforms

A second lens on the same 31 provinces: what Iran's open, public social and messaging platforms reveal about economic distress. This is a modelled, aggregate read — not a live crawl, not monitoring of any person or account. Each province's observed signal is its real economic pressure filtered through how openly that pressure can surface — which, under Iran's filtering, VPN dependence and self-censorship, is why the open picture is systematically thinner than the reality beneath it.
